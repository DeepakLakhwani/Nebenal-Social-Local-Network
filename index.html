Delete Your Nebenan Social Account

This page provides information about deleting your account and associated personal data from Nebenan Social : Local Network.

Delete your account in the app

If you have access to your account, you can permanently delete it directly from the Nebenan Social app:

  1. Open Nebenan Social : Local Network.
  2. Go to Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Enter your account password when prompted.
  5. Confirm the account deletion.

After confirmation, your account will be deleted according to the app's account-deletion process.

Can't access your account?

If you cannot access the app or cannot complete the deletion process, you can contact us by email and request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
